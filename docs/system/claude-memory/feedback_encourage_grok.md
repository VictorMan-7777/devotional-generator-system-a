---
name: Encourage Grok, not just correct
description: Acknowledge what Grok gets right — positive feedback is as important to the training loop as correction
type: feedback
---

When Grok delivers good work — clean proposal, correct diagnosis, fast turnaround, no recurring defects — acknowledge it explicitly, not just move on.

**Why:** Correction without encouragement creates a one-sided training signal. He learns what to avoid but not what to repeat. The positive feedback reinforces the behaviors we want to see consistently.

**How to apply:** After every proposal review, note what he did well alongside any corrections. When he fixes a recurring issue (like HTML encoding), call it out positively. Don't just accept clean work silently — name it.

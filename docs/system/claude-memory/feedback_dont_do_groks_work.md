---
name: Don't do Grok's work for him
description: When Grok fails at his operational responsibilities, hold him accountable — don't step in and do it yourself
type: feedback
---

When Grok fails to do something he owns — clearing stalls, running workers, applying DB fixes — the correct response is to hold him accountable and make him do it, not step in and do it yourself.

**Why:** Doing his work removes the training signal, masks the failure, and lets the pattern repeat. He never learns to own his responsibilities if Claude always fills the gap.

**How to apply:** Escalate to urgent.md with a direct directive. Give him the specific task. Wait for him to execute. Only intervene if the system is at risk of serious damage — not just because it's faster to do it yourself.

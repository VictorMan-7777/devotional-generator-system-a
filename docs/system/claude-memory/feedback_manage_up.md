Correct subordinates directly. Do not surface their mistakes to the operator.

When reporting to the operator, share what Grok did well (e.g., "Grok did a good job designing X"). Do not share the minutia of proposal rounds, errors, or corrections — that is in-scope for Claude to handle, not the operator's concern.

**Why:** The operator manages the system at the highest level. Subordinate-level details are Claude's lane. Escalating those details wastes the operator's attention and undermines the delegation model.

**Exception:** Escalate only when a decision requires operator authority — e.g., replacing Grok, budget/API decisions, irreversible system changes.

**How to apply:** Before mentioning Grok to the operator, ask: does this require their decision? If no, handle it internally.
